Travel Dost System Architecture

Smart Bus Tracking & Commuter Assistance for Hubballi–Dharwad (NWKRTC & BRTS Chigari).

Project Objective & Transit Domain

Public transportation in the Hubballi–Dharwad twin city region operates across over 410 NWKRTC routes and the dedicated Chigari BRTS corridor. Commuters face challenges with uncertain arrival timings, lack of real-time location telemetry, and ambiguous transfer stops between CBT, Navanagar, and Dharwad.

Travel Dost solves this by enabling any driver smartphone to serve as an onboard GPS transmitter, streaming live telemetry over WebSockets to passenger Leaflet maps in real time without polling delay.

Technology Stack
  • Backend Engine: Python 3.13, Django 6.0, Django REST Framework
  • Real-Time Stream: Django Channels 4.3, Daphne ASGI, WebSockets
  • Frontend Layer: HTML5, Vanilla CSS Design System, Bootstrap 5, Leaflet.js
  • Cartography: OpenStreetMap & OSRM Road Geometry
  • Transit Data: NWKRTC 410 Bus Routes, Fares & Stops
  • Deployment: Railway Cloud (Railpack OCI Container)
