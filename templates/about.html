Travel Dost System Architecture

Smart Bus Tracking & Travel Assistance System for Hubballi–Dharwad Region (NWKRTC).

Project Objective & Overview

Public transportation in the Hubballi–Dharwad twin city region relies heavily on NWKRTC city buses and the Chigari BRTS corridor. Passengers frequently face uncertainty regarding bus arrival timings, location, and optimal connecting routes.

Travel Dost solves this problem by turning any smartphone into an onboard GPS tracking transmitter, broadcasting real-time bus coordinates via WebSockets to passenger Leaflet maps without requiring manual page refreshes.
